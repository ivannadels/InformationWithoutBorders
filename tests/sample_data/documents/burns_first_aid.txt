First Aid and Initial Management of Burns

Synthetic test text for software testing only. Not clinical guidance.

Stop the burning process and cool the burn with clean running water for about twenty minutes. Remove jewellery and clothing that is not stuck to the skin.

Estimate the burned body surface area. Large burns require intravenous fluids, pain control and referral to a surgical facility.

Cover burns with a clean dressing and assess for inhalation injury in burns that occurred in enclosed spaces.
