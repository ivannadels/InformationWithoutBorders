Uncomplicated and Severe Malaria in Children

Synthetic test text for software testing only. Not clinical guidance.

Malaria should be suspected in any child with fever living in or returning from an endemic area. Confirm with a rapid diagnostic test or microscopy before treatment where possible.

Uncomplicated malaria is treated with artemisinin-based combination therapy according to national protocol.

Severe malaria includes impaired consciousness, convulsions, respiratory distress, severe anaemia or inability to drink. It requires parenteral treatment and urgent referral.
