Measles spreads rapidly in crowded displacement camps. Mass vaccination campaigns target children from 6 months to 14 years depending on the epidemiological situation.

Plan vaccination teams, vaccine quantities, the cold chain and safe injection practices. Vitamin A supplementation is often combined with the campaign.

Record coverage and investigate suspected cases with fever and rash.
