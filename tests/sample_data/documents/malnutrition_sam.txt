Severe Acute Malnutrition in Children: Outpatient and Inpatient Care

Synthetic test text for software testing only. Not clinical guidance.

Severe acute malnutrition (SAM) in children aged 6 to 59 months is identified by mid-upper arm circumference, weight-for-height or bilateral pitting oedema.

Children with an appetite and without medical complications can be treated as outpatients with ready-to-use therapeutic food. Children with complications or no appetite are admitted for inpatient stabilisation.

Dehydration in malnourished children is difficult to assess and rehydration must be slower and more careful than in well-nourished children, because of the risk of fluid overload. A special rehydration solution for malnutrition is used.

Monitor weight gain, treat infections and follow up after discharge.
