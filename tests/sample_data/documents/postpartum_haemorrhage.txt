Prevention and Management of Postpartum Haemorrhage

Synthetic test text for software testing only. Not clinical guidance.

Postpartum haemorrhage is heavy bleeding after childbirth and a major cause of maternal death.

Active management of the third stage of labour helps prevent it. When it occurs, call for help, massage the uterus, give uterotonic medication per protocol, and start intravenous fluids.

Refer to a facility with surgical capacity and blood transfusion if bleeding continues.
